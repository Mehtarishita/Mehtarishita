## How I Approach Product

</div>

<table width="100%">
<tr>
<td width="20%" valign="top" align="center">

**1. Discover**

Talk to users, read feedback, benchmark competitors

</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top" align="center">

**2. Define**

Turn the problem into clear requirements and a north star metric

</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top" align="center">

**3. Prioritize**

Rank ideas with RICE and cut scope to a realistic first version

</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top" align="center">

**4. Prototype**

Build something clickable so people can react to it early

</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top" align="center">

**5. Measure**

Plan experiments, track metrics and set guardrails

</td>
</tr>
</table>

**[Paytm Business Repeat Pay](https://rishita-product-portfolio.vercel.app/case-studies/paytm)**
`Paytm Innovation Challenge 2026 · Payments`

Merchants rarely use Paytm for business payments. Designed a repeat-payment flow where they save a payee, pay again, add a reference and share proof of payment. Set a north star metric with guardrails on duplicate payments, wrong recipients and pending time. Tested on a simulated survey, so real merchant interviews are the next step. An independent concept, not an official Paytm product.

`Product Growth · Gen Z Music Discovery`

**[Vasudha: Digital Livestock Safety](https://rishita-product-portfolio.vercel.app/case-studies/vasudha)**
`SIH 2025 · National Runner-Up among 1200+ teams`

Livestock antibiotic use in India has no reliable digital record, so unsafe milk and meat can reach buyers. Vasudha puts farmers, vets, labs, regulators and buyers on one shared, QR-traceable record, with a rule engine that works out safe drug withdrawal periods. Owned the research and documentation and built the backend and rule engine.

![Requirements](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Stakeholder_Requirements-B14EFF?style=flat-square)

**[Hitwicket Product Challenge](https://rishita-product-portfolio.vercel.app/case-studies/hitwicket)**
`Gaming · User Research`

Three problems: new players struggle to understand the game, social features are thin, and women are underrepresented. Proposed guided onboarding, Hitwicket Spotlight, a social prediction layer around big matches, and trust-first changes for women gamers such as fairer matchmaking and women-focused leagues. Built a clickable prototype of Spotlight. Based on five player interviews and a review of women gamers' feedback.
